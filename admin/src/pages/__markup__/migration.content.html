WordPress Migration

Import media, products, and pages from WordPress/WooCommerce exports.

Step 1: Media Library

Upload WordPress media export XML. Images will be downloaded and re-hosted.

Step 2: Products(Optional — recommended before pages)

Upload WooCommerce product CSV export. Supports Romanian & English column headers.

Step 3: Pages

Upload WordPress pages export XML. Content and comments will be imported.

Imported pages get the URL /<WordPress post name>, stored exactly as WordPress had it; the permalink's folder structure (for example /2024/05/) is not kept. Pages whose URL does not follow this site's slug rules are marked REVIEW SLUG in Content — check them after the import.
